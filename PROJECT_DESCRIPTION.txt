PROJECT TITLE: ResearchMate – AI Research Paper Assistant

OBJECTIVE:
To develop an AI-powered research paper assistant using Google Gemini API.

TECHNOLOGIES:
- React
- TypeScript
- Node.js
- Express
- Google Gemini API

FEATURES:
1. Abstract generation
2. Introduction generation
3. Literature review generation
4. Research objectives and questions
5. Research gap identification
6. Research methodology suggestions
7. Conclusion generation
8. Section regeneration
9. Document reader
10. Citation and ethics guidance
11. Manuscript export

PROMPT ENGINEERING:
Few-Shot Prompting is implemented through input-output examples in the Gemini system instruction.

PURPOSE:
To help students and researchers generate structured academic research content efficiently.
